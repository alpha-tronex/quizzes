Privacy Policy — Quiz Master

Last updated: September 23, 2026

What This App Does

Quiz Master lets students in a learning program take quizzes assigned by their instructor, view their scores, and review a history of past attempts. Quizzes are organized into cohorts (classes), and only quizzes assigned to your cohort are shown to you. An account is required to use the app.

Data We Collect

DataWhyHow long
Name, username, email, phone To create and identify your account. Email and phone are optional. Until your account is deleted
Password To authenticate you when you sign in Stored as a one-way hash — we never store or can view your actual password
Quiz results (answers, score, time taken, completion date) To show your quiz history and progress over time Until your account is deleted
Cohort assignment To determine which quizzes are shown to you Until your account is deleted

We do not collect your device identifiers, precise location, contacts, or photos.

How Your Data Is Used

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show you the quizzes assigned to your cohort and your own quiz history.
  • Your name, contact information, and quiz results are visible to the administrators (instructors) of your cohort, so they can assign quizzes, review your progress, and manage your account.

We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not share it with any third party for marketing purposes.

Data Storage

  • Your data is stored in a private database that we operate ourselves.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-tracking SDKs in this app.
  • We do not share your data with any outside company.

Third-Party Services

Quiz Master does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or AI service. The app communicates only with our own server to sign you in, load your quizzes, and save your results.

Account and Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account and the data associated with it, contact your program administrator or email us at the address below. We will delete your account and quiz history within a reasonable time of a verified request.

Children's Privacy

Quiz Master accounts are created for students enrolled in a supervised learning program by that program's administrator. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children outside of that supervised context.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
